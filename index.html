About Us

At Mirissa Whales Watching Tours, we offer unforgettable ocean adventures in the heart of Sri Lanka’s southern coast. Experience the thrill of witnessing magnificent blue whales, sperm whales, dolphins, and other marine life in their natural habitat while enjoying the beauty of the Indian Ocean.

With experienced guides, safe and comfortable boats, and years of expertise in whale watching, we are dedicated to providing you with a memorable and responsible marine experience. Whether you are a nature lover, photographer, or adventure seeker, our tours are designed to create moments you will never forget.
